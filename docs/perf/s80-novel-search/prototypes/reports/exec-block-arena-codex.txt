The patch appears to preserve execution outputs, but the arena does not recycle pass-A position caches when no-merge is disabled and can retain excessive scratch-map memory after high-cardinality blocks.

- [P2] Recycle merged plan caches when no-merge is disabled — /home/oz/projects/wt/novel-s80/proto/exec-block-arena/crates/torus-bridge/src/native_executor.rs:11773-11773
  When `TORUS_S80_EXEC_ARENA=1` and `TORUS_SETTLE_NO_MERGE=0`, pass A takes recycled caches here, but Pass B consumes and drops each cache in `merge_disjoint`; only `pos_runs` are returned to the pool at the end of the call, and that list is empty in this mode. Successful calls therefore never return these pass-A caches to `POSITION_CACHES` and keep reallocating them; the fallback path also drops its plans. Recycle plan caches after merging or abandoning them.

- [P2] Bound total retained recycler capacity — /home/oz/projects/wt/novel-s80/proto/exec-block-arena/crates/torus-core/src/recycle.rs:28-28
  After a high-cardinality block touching many markets, each free list can retain up to 1,024 maps with capacities up to 65,536; `clear()` keeps their bucket storage, so the position caches alone can pin tens of millions of position and dirty-map buckets, in addition to the other pools. This can leave the process holding many gigabytes after a burst. Add an aggregate retained-byte or capacity budget rather than relying only on per-map and per-list limits.
