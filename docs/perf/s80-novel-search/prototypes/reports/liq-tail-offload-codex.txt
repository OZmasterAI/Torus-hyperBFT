  When the loop reaches `act`, later candidates are not valued on the serial path, but this eager collection still calls `liq_view` for the whole scan window. A tail candidate can latch a local read failure in `ctx.reader_fault` (for example via `AccountReader::mark`), which `take_fatal_error` treats as fatal, making the block fail because of an account the loop never needed to read. Avoid recording faults from discarded pre-values or restrict pre-evaluation to candidates whose results will be consumed.
