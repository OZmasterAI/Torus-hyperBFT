# s74 — RocksDB WAL cap: crash-restart A/B (item 4 Part A)

**Verdict: adopt.** `TORUS_ROCKSDB_MAX_TOTAL_WAL_MB` now defaults to 512 MiB
(`0` restores RocksDB's automatic threshold). The restarted node's DB open drops
from 20.5 s to 7.9 s, consistent in every cell. The chain freeze shrinks less,
because the remaining wait is the view-sync gap (Part B).

## Change

- 51e42c1 ports the node-side part of b0a57ce (opt-in knob,
  `Options::set_max_total_wal_size`) plus its tests (`tests/wal_budget.rs`:
  parser, persisted OPTIONS, automatic oldest-WAL flush, SIGKILL recovery).
  The fresh-DB harness restrictions of b0a57ce are not ported.
- The follow-up commit makes 512 MiB the default. Unset or invalid gives
  512 MiB, `0` gives automatic, `N` gives N MiB.
- Why: with 44 CFs × 128 MiB × 4 buffers, RocksDB's automatic cap is
  about 86 GiB, so a cold CF pins every WAL segment since open.

## Campaign `~/bench-results-matched/s74-wal-20260928`

- Frozen binary: `artifacts/wal` (51e42c1, node sha 1f8b16a1), same binary in
  both arms.
- Env: s73 arms plus `TORUS_ROCKSDB_MAX_TOTAL_WAL_MB` = 0 (off) or 512 (cap).
- Cells: 300 s, cap 200, 10 markets, `--crash-at 60` (SIGKILL val1, restart
  from the same data dir). One warm-up cell first, then ABBA order:
  off, cap, cap, off, off, cap.
- Each node's RocksDB `LOG` is kept in `<cell>/rocksdb-log/`.
- Analysis: `analyze.py`.

- Freeze = the longest val0 no-commit gap overlapping the kill (1 s sampler).

- **DB open is −12.6 s** and does not overlap between arms (off 19.6–20.9 s,
  cap 7.6–8.2 s).
- **The LOGs confirm the mechanism.** Without the cap, every restart replays
  from WAL `#4`, the first file after open. With the cap it replays 5 files.
- **Freeze is noisy with the cap (16–35 s).** After the DB opens, the rejoiner
  still waits for the survivors' doubled view timeout before it can vote.
  That is Part B (rejoin view sync).
- **Disk:** a 300 s cell ends at about 4–5 GiB per cell with the cap, against
  31 GiB without it. Sizes come from the prune step's deleted bytes. Retained
  WAL was most of the old footprint.
- **matched/s was not scored.** Host noise was present: another session was
  running `du` and `btop` during cap-r1. The numbers show no regression; the
  cap arm is higher in all three pairs.
- **All crash cells exit 2 and read REJECT.** The survivors' stall exceeds the
  30 s liveness rule, and val1 reads UNKNOWN (known harness gap). Agreement
  and the crash gate pass in every cell.

## Not done / follow-ups

- 7.9 s remains in DB open. Five WAL files of up to 512 MiB are replayed at
  about 124 MB/s. A 256 MiB cap, or `avoid_flush_during_recovery`, could
  shave a few seconds. Not measured.
- Part B (view sync on rejoin) is the larger freeze lever.
